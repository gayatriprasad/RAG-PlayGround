# Incident Response Process

## Severity Levels

### SEV-1 (Critical)
- Complete service outage affecting >50% of users
- Data breach or security compromise
- Revenue-impacting failure (payments, billing)
- Response time: 5 minutes to acknowledge, 15 minutes to war room

### SEV-2 (High)
- Partial outage or severe degradation
- Single critical service down with workaround available
- Data integrity issue (not breach)
- Response time: 15 minutes to acknowledge, 30 minutes to investigation

### SEV-3 (Medium)
- Minor feature degradation
- Non-critical service issue
- Performance degradation within SLA but trending wrong
- Response time: 1 hour to acknowledge, 4 hours to investigation

## Incident Workflow

### 1. Detection
- Automated: PagerDuty alert from Datadog monitors
- Manual: Anyone can declare via `/incident` Slack command in #incidents

### 2. Triage
- On-call engineer assesses severity within 5 minutes
- Creates incident channel: #inc-YYYY-MM-DD-short-description
- Posts initial assessment using incident template

### 3. Response
- SEV-1/SEV-2: Incident Commander (IC) assigned (senior on-call)
- IC coordinates response, delegates tasks, manages communications
- Status updates every 15 minutes (SEV-1) or 30 minutes (SEV-2)
- Customer-facing status page updated within 10 minutes of confirmation

### 4. Resolution
- Fix deployed and verified
- Monitoring confirms recovery
- All-clear posted to incident channel
- Status page updated to "Resolved"

### 5. Post-Incident
- Blameless post-mortem within 48 hours (SEV-1/SEV-2)
- Post-mortem within 1 week (SEV-3)
- Template: docs.internal.acme.com/templates/postmortem
- Action items tracked in JIRA project INCIDENT
- Post-mortem shared in #engineering and archived in Confluence

## Communication Templates

### Internal Update
```
🚨 [SEV-X] [Service Name] — [Brief Description]
Status: Investigating / Identified / Monitoring / Resolved
Impact: [Who is affected and how]
Next update: [Time]
IC: [Name]
```

### External Status Page
```
[Investigating/Identified/Monitoring/Resolved]: [Service Name]
We are aware of issues affecting [description].
[X]% of users may experience [impact].
We are actively working on a resolution.
Next update in [X] minutes.
```

## Escalation Path
1. On-call engineer (immediate)
2. Engineering Manager (15 min no response)
3. Director of Engineering (30 min SEV-1)
4. VP Engineering (1 hour unresolved SEV-1)
5. CTO (2 hours unresolved SEV-1)

## Tools
- PagerDuty: alerting and on-call schedules
- Datadog: monitoring and dashboards
- Slack: coordination (#incidents, incident channels)
- StatusPage: external communication
- JIRA: action item tracking
- Confluence: post-mortem storage
